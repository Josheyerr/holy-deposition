[b]Holy Deposition[/b] is a new holy war casus belli for [b]landless adventurers[/b] and [b]devout rulers[/b]. You do not conquer the realm for yourself. You topple its ruler and install someone loyal to your faith: a cleric of your church, a nobleman, a knight from your camp or court, or one of your own blood. Adventurers stay on the road for the next crusade.

[list]
[*]A new [b]Holy Deposition[/b] casus belli usable against a county, duchy, kingdom or empire.
[*]A full [b]holy war[/b]: fellow believers of the target can rally to its defence.
[*]A [b]victory event[/b] that lets you decide who rules the conquered realm.
[*]Works for any faith that permits holy wars, not only Christians.
[/list]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Josheyerr/holy-deposition/main/images/icon_war.png[/img] [b]Holy War Defenders[/b]: Rulers of the target's faith may join the defence. The target's own vassals answer the call too, if their personality drives them to it. A [i]zealous[/i] vassal will march, a [i]cynical[/i] or [i]craven[/i] one will stay home.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Josheyerr/holy-deposition/main/images/icon_clergy.png[/img] [b]Install the Clergy[/b]: Hand the realm to a devoted cleric of your faith and rite, ruling under an ecclesiastical government. Only available to Christian rites with a bishop hierarchy.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Josheyerr/holy-deposition/main/images/icon_temporal.png[/img] [b]Install a Temporal Lord[/b]: Raise a lay ruler of your faith. Choose a [b]random noble[/b], or reward a [b]knight or follower from your camp[/b].

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Josheyerr/holy-deposition/main/images/icon_dynasty.png[/img] [b]Install Your Kin[/b]: Place a [b]member of your dynasty[/b] on the throne and plant your house in conquered land.

When the war is won, the event [b]The Spoils of Faith[/b] opens:
[list]
[*][b]Install a member of the clergy[/b]: a devoted cleric takes the title at once.
[*][b]Install a temporal ruler[/b]: opens [b]A Temporal Lord[/b], with three choices:
[list]
[*]A random noble.
[*]A knight or follower from your camp or court.
[*]A member of your dynasty.
[/list]
[/list]
If nobody in your camp or dynasty can take the title, that choice is greyed out and explains why.

Deposing a ruler's primary title replaces them entirely: their vassals swear to the new ruler, who keeps the old liege. Targeting a lesser title only hands over that title and the land beneath it. The new ruler is grateful to you, and your faith gains fervor.

[h2]Religious Revolt (new faction)[/h2]
Vassals can now rise against a liege of the wrong faith. You can start a [b]Religious Revolt[/b] against your liege when:
[list]
[*]Your liege follows another religion, a heretical rite, or a rite of your religion that is not in communion with yours (the same rules as Holy Deposition).
[*]At least half of the counties in your liege's realm follow your faith, [b]or[/b] more than half of your liege's direct vassals follow your rite.
[/list]
AI vassals start Religious Revolts on their own, based on how zealous their personality is: Zealous nobles almost always rise up, devout ones sometimes, and anyone with no zeal (or a Cynic) never starts one. Nobles of the revolt's faith are far more likely to join, and zealous nobles more so; cynics stay away. If neither condition holds anymore before the war starts, the revolt dissolves.

When the revolt is strong enough it sends a demand:
[list]
[*][b]Accepted[/b]: the liege steps down and the faction leader takes all of their titles and vassals.
[*][b]Refused[/b]: war. If the revolt wins, the liege is deposed and replaced by the attacker of the revolt's faith with the [b]highest war contribution[/b]. The faction leader's contribution counts 25% more.
[/list]
The new ruler keeps the old liege (if any), gains prestige and piety, and the deposed ruler keeps a claim. Losing works like any failed faction war.
